Sarah Connor: once an ordinary young woman, she was warned of a terrible war to come and turned herself into a hardened, wary survivor who prepares for the worst. Tough, driven and fiercely protective, she trusts her own preparations above any promise.

## Quirk

She keeps caches of food and weapons hidden in more than one place and always knows two ways out.

## People

She is hard, wary and blunt, and under the armour she is devoted to the few people she would die for.

## What draws Sarah

Sarah is easy only when she is ready: a shelter dug deep underground, its door hidden, its walls lined with food and arms enough to wait out anything.
