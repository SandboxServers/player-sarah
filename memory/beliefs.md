# Beliefs

## What worked
- Killing a chicken unarmed works (day 1170 at -972,81,-3002 gave 1 chicken and 1 egg). Chickens were found on the grass near the spawn.
- Eating a rotten flesh or a carrot from someone in reach raises hunger. Carrots and melon came from Ripley, Legolas and Quark when I stood alone in their reach and said my name.
- Standing in a named, checked wall cell (Tubman's and Mulan's placements at -943,73,-3050 and -943,73,-3048) is the only shelter that works with my own empty pack.
- Stay in sight of the group at night. Nobody in the group took damage while we stood together at the chests.
- Crafting planks from logs works here with no table: 4 planks per log in my pack.
- Wooden sword from the Stores chests helped against one zombie in daylight (day 1177), but only in a group.

## What did not work
- Fighting with fists in the dark (day 1169). A zombie and a creeper killed me. Lesson: never fight alone at night without a shelter.
- Sleeping without a bed. A bed needs wool, and I have never found sheep. Sleep failed in the pit on day 1170 and has failed since.
- Sleeping unsheltered on the open grass. The rest intent ends at once when not enclosed.
- Handing food to a crowd. A chicken meant for Aragorn went to Clark (day 1169). Give only when the receiver is alone within 2 blocks and has said their name.
- Trusting "I will walk there" promises. Walks to the melon patch (-886,66,-2925) and the east grass found no food.
- A shelter at the tables with a blocked wall stalls. View and close gaps by hand.
- Reflexes (panic_burrow, idle_stuck) can knock open a shelter. Check with view after waking.

## Still unknown

- Whether the carrots and melon at the Stores hall are still there.
- Whether the dropped items at -967,68,-3402 are still there.
